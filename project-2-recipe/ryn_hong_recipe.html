★ How To Make A Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwich ★

🥜🍞🍇

Hi everyone!! This is my first website ever lol
Today i am going to teach you how to make a PB&J!!!!
Its sooo easy if i can do it u can do it too

!!! WARNING: dont eat this if ur allergic to peanuts !!!

📝 What You Need

ThingHow Many
Bread 🍞2 slices
Peanut Butter 🥜a lot (however much u want)
Jam or Jelly 🍇a lot (grape is the best)
Butter Knife 🔪1
Plate1 (or a cutting board if u dont have one)

👩‍🍳 How To Make It

Step 1. Take out 2 slices of bread and put them side by side on the plate. MAKE SURE to close the bread bag or it gets hard

Step 2. Use the knife to scoop peanut butter and spread it on one slice. Go all the way to the edges!! If u only do the middle the edges taste bad

Step 3. Wipe the knife first (or else peanut butter gets in the jelly jar ;-;) then spread jelly on the other slice

Step 4. Put the two slices together with the spread sides facing each other. SPREAD SIDE GOES INSIDE!!! not outside

Step 5. Cut it in half. If u cut it diagonally it makes triangles and it looks more yummy (this is science)

Step 6. EAT IT!!!!!! 😋😋😋

💡 Pro Tips

  • Toasting the bread makes it even better
  • Put a thin layer of peanut butter on BOTH slices and the jelly in the middle so the bread doesnt get soggy
  • You can add banana slices too 👍
  • Best with a glass of milk
